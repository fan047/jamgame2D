# 首批音效素材来源

八条交付文件在 `Assets/Holedown/Audio`，均为 48 kHz、16-bit、单声道 WAV。Unity 场景 `SampleScene` 的 `Feedback` 对象绑定了这八条音效，可在 Inspector 替换对应素材。

| 交付文件 | 原文件与作者 | 处理 | 授权 |
|---|---|---|---|
| `volley_launch.wav` | `air_move.wav`，Almitory | 剪取较短的气流片段，加速并收尾 | CC0 1.0 · [Air Woosh Move](https://opengameart.org/content/air-woosh-move) |
| `block_hit.wav` | `click_2.wav`，Robin Lamb | 截取高频轻敲的起音 | CC0 1.0 · [UI Sound Effects](https://opengameart.org/content/ui-sound-effects-button-clicks-user-feedback-notifications) |
| `block_break.wav` | `glass_breaking.wav`，Till Behrend，由 TinyWorlds 上传 | 选取开头的碎裂及碎片声，缩短尾音 | CC0 1.0 · [Glass Break](https://opengameart.org/content/glass-break) |
| `reward_break.wav` | `jingles_STEEL16.ogg`，Kenney | 加速两声上扬音并淡出 | CC0 1.0 · [85 Short Music Jingles](https://opengameart.org/content/85-short-music-jingles) |
| `stage_clear.wav` | `jingles_STEEL10.ogg`，Kenney | 稍加速，保留上行乐句 | CC0 1.0 · [85 Short Music Jingles](https://opengameart.org/content/85-short-music-jingles) |
| `stage_fail.wav` | `jingles_STEEL11.ogg`，Kenney | 稍放慢并压低音量，保留下行乐句 | CC0 1.0 · [85 Short Music Jingles](https://opengameart.org/content/85-short-music-jingles) |
| `manual_recall.wav` | `air_move.wav`，Almitory | 剪取气流片段并倒放 | CC0 1.0 · [Air Woosh Move](https://opengameart.org/content/air-woosh-move) |
| `ui_confirm.wav` | `click_3.wav`，Robin Lamb | 剪短并降低峰值 | CC0 1.0 · [UI Sound Effects](https://opengameart.org/content/ui-sound-effects-button-clicks-user-feedback-notifications) |
